📱 Phone self-check

Three quick checks to make sure this phone is ready to jam with friends. Each check only runs when you tap its button. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded — the mic and camera are only listened to for a moment, on this phone, and then switched off.

🎤 Microphone

Checks that this site can hear your microphone. When the check runs, say something out loud or tap near the mic — you'll see the green bar jump.

Listening… make some noise!

📷 Camera

Checks that this site can open your front camera. You'll see yourself in the preview — tap "Done" when you've had a look and the camera switches off.

⏱️ Audio delay

Estimates how long this phone takes to get sound out, using the phone's own audio numbers. Under about 80 ms keeps everyone in time when jamming together.

Heads-up: if a check asks for permission, your browser will pop up a request — that's normal, and saying yes only applies while this page is open. If all three pass, your phone is ready for a session.
